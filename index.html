How Halcyan Works

Three steps to better water — no plumber required

01

Water Enters

Hard water from your mains flows into the compact Halcyan unit, installed on your incoming pipe. No electricity needed — it runs purely on water pressure.

02

The Science Happens

Inside, a unique alloy core transforms calcium crystals through catalytic conversion. No chemicals, no power, no moving parts — just proven science that's been used worldwide for over 30 years.

03

Better Water Flows

Conditioned water flows throughout your entire home. New limescale is prevented from forming. Old limescale buildup starts to dissolve. Your taps, shower, kettle, and appliances all benefit.
